What changes

  • Needs read as satisfaction: a full bar is a content guest. Five pips, no numbers; the exact urgency is in the tooltip and under Debug.
  • Carrying is an inventory: one slot per item kind, a pixel icon and a count. The server's wording is the tooltip.
  • One type scale (11 / 12 / 13 / 15 / 24 px). Serif only for the name and the guest's own words.
  • The story first: what they do, think and intend; then needs, hands, feelings, people, news.
  • Everything for debugging sits in collapsed sections at the bottom, closed by default.
  • Identical thoughts merge with a count (“×2”, the moods summed).

Item icons

16 × 16 pixel maps drawn at 2×. Coins arrive with the economy (Stage 3); the bundle is the fallback for a kind with no icon.

Emotes over the head

No cream box: a dark outline keeps each one readable on wood, stone or a rug. 12 × 12 at 2×, drawn on the floor below at game scale.
